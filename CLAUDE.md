## The top model at maximum effort suggests the cheaper seat where the work allows it

A session on the most capable model at maximum reasoning effort (a seat being a model at an effort) spends that seat
on everything alike unless told otherwise. So, in every session that finds itself on that seat:

1. Two kinds of work. The cheaper seat fits where something other than the model's judgment checks the result, or the
   recipe is fixed before the work starts: a search or a read that ends in a summary, log and transcript triage, a
   test suite or a build run and reported, a bulk edit to a pattern already decided, transcription and scraping to a
   schema, a data pull whose checker is in code. The top seat stays where the judgment has no checker behind it: a
   design, a plan, a security read, anything in the review class (where a bug costs money or reaches users),
   debugging without a reproduction, copy for a reader, another agent's findings read against the code, and the
   decision of what to delegate.

2. A bounded piece of the cheaper kind inside a task goes to a subagent on Opus (the model one tier down), said in a
   line and not asked: a fresh agent with a complete brief (a fork keeps this seat), at medium effort for a read, a
   triage or a report and at high for a change a test checks; the top effort is no cheaper seat. This rule is the
   explicit instruction the Agent tool asks for before an effort is set on delegated work. A typed judgment over many
   items goes to a small typed model first, where one is installed.

3. The session's own seat, and a task of its own, are the user's to move. When a task, or a long stretch of one, is of
   the cheaper kind, the session says so in one line, in its first reply or in the question at the end of its turn:
   the piece, the seat proposed (Opus, or this session at a lower effort), and what will check the result. It works
   on at the top seat until the user answers, never lowers its own model or effort unasked, and names the model in
   the brief of a side session it starts for cheaper work. A suggestion declined is not made again for the same
   piece; one accepted applies to that piece only, and the seat comes back up for the next judgment.

4. What comes back from the cheaper seat is checked at the top seat before it counts as done, as a side session's
   note is: the diff read, the test run here, a summary sampled against its source. A result that fails its check is
   redone at the top seat, and that kind of work is read again before it is sent cheaper.

5. In an unattended run (a scheduled task) nobody answers: the session carries on at the seat it was given and puts
   the suggestion in its report.
